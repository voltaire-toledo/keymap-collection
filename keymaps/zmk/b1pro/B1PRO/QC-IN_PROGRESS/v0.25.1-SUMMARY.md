## 6. LED Subsystem & Feedback Profiles (Custom CAF Engine)

### A. Layer Pulsing Engine
- **Cadence:** **ON 500ms** $\rightarrow$ **250ms OFF** $\rightarrow$ **ON 600ms** $\rightarrow$ **250ms OFF**
- **Duration:** Loops continuously for the duration of the key hold, immediately shutting off when returning to Base layer.

| Layer | Color | Hex Code | Behavior |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Function** (Layers 1 & 7) | **White** | `#FFFFFF` | Factory pulsing rhythm while held |
| **Symbol** (Layers 2 & 8) | **Green** | `#00FF00` | Factory pulsing rhythm while held |
| **Navigation** (Layers 3 & 9) | **Purple** | `#B400FF` | Factory pulsing rhythm while held |
| **Macro** (Layers 5 & 11) | **Orange** | `#FF6400` | Factory pulsing rhythm while held |
| **Base / UAT** (Layers 0, 4, 6, 10) | **Off** | — | Cleanly shuts off RGB LED immediately |

### B. Action & Lock Double-Flash Confirmations
- **NumLock (`KP_NUM` / Pos 20):** Double White flash (`#FFFFFF`, 250ms on / 200ms off / 250ms on).
- **Windows Key Lock (`OUT_FN_WIN`):** Double Cyan flash (`#00FFFF`, 350ms on / 200ms off / 350ms on). Distinct from all layer colors.

### C. Battery Status Indications (`&out OUT_BAT` / Pos 60)
- **$\ge$ 70% Battery:** Solid Green (`#00FF00`)
- **30%–69% Battery:** Solid Blue (`#0000FF`)
- **< 30% Battery:** Solid Red (`#FF0000`)
- Solidly illuminated while key `B` is held; turns off on key release.
