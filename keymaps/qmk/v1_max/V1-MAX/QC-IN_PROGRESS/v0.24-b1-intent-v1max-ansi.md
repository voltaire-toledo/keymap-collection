## Required V1 invariants retained

- QMK layers 0 and 2 remain `MAC_BASE` and `WIN_BASE`, respectively, so
  `v1_max.c` DIP switching retains its stock layer contract.
- The vendor `BT_HST1`, `BT_HST2`, `BT_HST3`, `P2P4G`, `BAT_LVL`, RGB, raw-HID
  / VIA policy, and encoder mappings are retained. The Keychron host controls
  select a host on press and use their built-in hold-to-pair behavior. Battery
  indication is at Fn+B; RGB controls are at Fn+Tab/Q/W/E/R/T/[ / ]/Quote and
  the Fn+PgUp/PgDn positions, with encoder press as RGB toggle.
- The V1 Max STM32 DFU recovery path remains independent of the candidate:
  Cable mode plus Esc while connecting, or the reset under Space.
- ANSI `LAYOUT_ansi_82` is the only physical matrix used. ISO and JIS are out
  of scope.

## Behavioral translation

| B1 behavior | QMK candidate |
| --- | --- |
| Mac/Windows bases plus function layers | `MAC_BASE`/`MAC_FUNC` at 0/1 and `WIN_BASE`/`WIN_FUNC` at 2/3 |
| Symbol, navigation, UAT, macro pages | Dedicated Mac and Windows layer groups 4-11; order changes only to preserve V1 DIP layers 0/2 |
| Home-row modifiers with bilateral rules | QMK mod-taps with `CHORDAL_HOLD` and per-key `PERMISSIVE_HOLD` |
| Tap Esc; hold 600 ms Caps Lock | `ESC_CAPS` custom keycode |
| Hold Fn; tap one-shot macro-selector | QMK tap dances `TD_FN_MAC` and `TD_FN_WIN`, 200 ms |
| Long-hold boot behavior | 3-second `TD_BOOT` invoking `QK_BOOT` from either Fn layer |
| Symbol tap dances | QMK tap dances for `-_`, `=+`, `()`, `{}`, `[]`, and `<>` |
| B1 mouse movement/wheel/buttons/acceleration | QMK Mouse Keys on the Mac and Windows navigation layers |
| B1 Bluetooth tap/double-tap controls | Native Keychron host keys; no separate QMK pairing dance |

## Explicit assumptions and exclusions

1. B1-specific `OUT_BLE`, `OUT_24G`, `OUT_CHG`, and `OUT_CHGD` are not user
   keycodes on the V1 Max. The candidate retains the vendor's native host,
   2.4 GHz, and battery controls instead.
2. The stock `MO(MAC_FN)` / `MO(WIN_FN)` positions are intentionally replaced
   with 200 ms tap dances. Hold selects the relevant Fn layer; tap activates a
   one-shot Macro selector that clears after its next non-modifier keypress or
   one second of inactivity.
   This is a changed behavior and requires timing-focused hardware QC.
3. Undefined B1 tokens `tap_dance_c_mac` and `tap_dance_v_mac` are interpreted
   as standard copy/paste on the Mac navigation layer. They do not establish a
   separate tap-dance requirement.
4. B1 `C_AC_SEARCH` is rendered as Spotlight (`Cmd+Space`) on Mac and
   `Win+S` on Windows. B1's labels do not identify a unique portable HID
   keycode.
5. The V1 has a different physical geometry, an encoder, and a navigation
   column. B1 positions are mapped by behavior group, not copied by index.
6. The UAT layers remain intentionally inert except for return-to-base, as in
   the B1 source; their purpose is not defined by the source file.
